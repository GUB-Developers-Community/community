# Events & Activities

Events help members learn together and turn ideas into practice. Activities can be online or in person and can be led by a student, a small team, or a guest contributor.

## Suggested Formats

- **Study session:** Work through a topic together and compare notes.
- **Workshop:** Follow a practical tutorial from setup to a small result.
- **Coding session:** Build a project collaboratively with focused tasks.
- **Project showcase:** Demonstrate work, explain lessons learned, and invite feedback.
- **Discussion:** Explore a technology, career path, or community topic.

## Planning Checklist

1. Choose a clear topic and audience.
2. Set a date, duration, location or meeting link, and capacity.
3. Share prerequisites and setup instructions early.
4. Assign hosts, facilitators, and a contact person.
5. Provide an accessible way to ask questions.
6. Collect feedback and publish useful follow-up notes.
