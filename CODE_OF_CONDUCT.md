# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

The GUB Developers Community is committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free environment for everyone, regardless of experience level, background, identity, or technical interests.

## Expected Behavior

Participants are expected to:

- Be respectful and considerate in discussions, reviews, events, and pull requests.
- Welcome beginners and explain concepts without belittling others.
- Give constructive feedback focused on ideas and work, not people.
- Assume good intent while addressing mistakes responsibly.
- Respect privacy and avoid sharing personal information without permission.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, intimidation, deliberate disruption, sexualized attention, doxxing, and sharing private information without consent are not acceptable. Content that violates applicable law or platform rules is also prohibited.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, contact the community maintainers through a private GitHub channel where possible. Include enough context to help us understand what happened, and avoid publicly reposting sensitive details.

Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical. We may remove content, restrict participation, or take other appropriate action.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies to community spaces, repositories, events, discussions, and other settings where someone is representing the GUB Developers Community.

## Good-Faith Participation

We expect reports and enforcement discussions to be made in good faith. Retaliation against someone who raises a genuine concern is not acceptable.
