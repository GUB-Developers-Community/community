# GUB Developers Community

**Learn together. Build together. Grow together.**

Welcome to the GUB Developers Community — a student-led community for developers, programmers, and technology enthusiasts at Green University of Bangladesh (GUB).

Our goal is to create a space where students can learn new technologies, collaborate on meaningful projects, share knowledge, and grow together as developers.

Whether you're writing your first line of code or building your next big project, you're welcome here.

> This is an independent, student-led initiative and is not officially affiliated with or endorsed by Green University of Bangladesh.

## About Us

We believe that the best way to become a better developer is by building things, sharing knowledge, and learning from one another.

Through this community, we aim to:

- Encourage students to learn programming and explore new technologies.
- Build real-world projects and contribute to open source.
- Help beginners get started with Git, GitHub, and collaborative development.
- Share learning resources, opportunities, and technical knowledge.
- Organize workshops, coding sessions, and community events.
- Create a supportive environment where everyone can grow.

## Who Can Join?

Any student of Green University of Bangladesh who is interested in programming, software development, or technology is welcome.

You don't need to be an expert or have previous open-source experience. Curiosity, willingness to learn, and a desire to contribute are all you need.

| Area | What to expect |

## How to Get Involved

There are several ways to participate:

1. Explore the repository and check out the available resources.
2. Browse [project ideas](projects/project-ideas.md) and find something you'd like to build.
3. Share your ideas or ask questions through GitHub Issues and Discussions.
4. Contribute to the community by improving documentation, sharing resources, or working on projects.
5. Participate in community events and collaborate with other students.

You can start with something as simple as correcting a typo or adding a useful learning resource.

## How to Contribute

We welcome contributions from developers of all experience levels.

1. Fork this repository.
2. Create a new branch for your contribution.
3. Make your changes.
4. Commit and push your changes to your fork.
5. Open a pull request explaining what you've changed.

Please read our [Contributing Guidelines](CONTRIBUTING.md) before submitting your first contribution.

All contributions should follow our [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).

## Our Community Values

We want this community to be a place where people feel comfortable asking questions, sharing ideas, and learning from mistakes.

We value:

- Collaboration over competition.
- Learning over perfection.
- Respectful communication.
- Open knowledge sharing.
- Consistent effort and mutual support.
